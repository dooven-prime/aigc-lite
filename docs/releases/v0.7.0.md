## Upgrade and rollback

The schema transition is `0020_knowledge_admission` →
`0025_invocation_timeout`. Before upgrading a real deployment, stop writers
and new external dispatch, record unresolved dispatch IDs, take a verified
database backup outside the working tree, and test the upgrade on a copy.
The tested representative copies preserved QualificationReceipt,
KnowledgeAdmissionReceipt, CurrentUseBinding, active Grant including its
`calls_used`, and `dispatching`/`indeterminate` Dispatch rows exactly. The
new authorization-invocation ledger starts empty for historical records; an
old unresolved external operation must still be reconciled, never retried
merely because migration succeeded.

Rollback means stopping `0.7.0`, restoring the **pre-upgrade database backup**
to a separately checked `v0.6.0` deployment, and reconciling any externally
executed work. Do not treat an in-place Alembic downgrade as a safe rollback
after `0.7.0` writes, or discard post-upgrade authority/dispatch history.

## Authority and research boundaries

Candidate storage, epistemic qualification, current knowledge use, and
execution authorization remain separate. Model output does not gain these
authorities by changing a JSON field, passing a verifier, or calling MCP.
The MDE casepack reports are conditional research diagnostics: an enrolled
signature authenticates bytes under configured keys, not the underlying
sample or publication history. They issue no QualificationReceipt,
CurrentUseBinding, or AuthorizationGrant.

`DisambiguationCertificate.v0` remains on the independent research branch
and is **not** in this tag or in the `0.1.0` casepack wheel. Math Project 003
still lacks a passing complete Comparator/kernel replay and statement
alignment acceptance. SandboxBroker remains without full OS-isolation
acceptance. ROS 2 simulator and narrow graph evaluation are not real
Nav2/Gazebo or physical-hardware acceptance. External enforcer receipts
attest configured signatures; they do not establish hardware or
organizational independence.
